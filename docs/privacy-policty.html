ExpiryMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

ExpiryMate is an expiration date tracking application designed to help users keep track of products, receive local reminders, and reduce waste.

This Privacy Policy explains how ExpiryMate handles information when you use the application.

1. Information Stored by the App

ExpiryMate allows users to enter information such as:

  • Item names
  • Expiration dates
  • Item categories
  • Reminder preferences
  • Optional notes
  • Item status, such as Active, Used, or Discarded

This information is stored locally on your device.

ExpiryMate does not require an account and does not send this information to developer-operated servers.

2. Data Collection

ExpiryMate does not collect personal data or user-created item data on developer-controlled servers.

The app does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Tracking SDKs
  • Firebase Analytics
  • User accounts
  • Cloud synchronization
  • Remote databases

ExpiryMate does not sell or share user data with third parties.

3. Notifications

ExpiryMate may request permission to send notifications.

Notifications are used only to remind users about upcoming expiration dates. Reminder scheduling is performed locally on the device.

ExpiryMate does not use notification permission to collect personal information.

4. Local Storage

Application data is stored locally using Android storage technologies, including a local Room database and application preferences.

Users can permanently remove locally stored ExpiryMate data by deleting items within the app where available, clearing the application's storage through Android settings, or uninstalling the application.

5. Android Backup

Depending on your Android device and backup settings, Android may automatically back up some ExpiryMate application data using the Android system backup service associated with your Google account.

This backup process is controlled by Android and Google, not by ExpiryMate or the developer.

Users can manage device backup settings through Android system settings.

6. Internet and External Services

The current version of ExpiryMate does not require internet access for its core functionality and does not transmit application data to developer-operated servers.

7. Data Sharing

ExpiryMate does not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Data entered into ExpiryMate remains stored locally on the user's device until the user deletes the relevant item, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the application.

The developer does not maintain a remote copy of this data.

Android system backups may be subject to Google's own backup retention policies.

9. Children's Privacy

ExpiryMate is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed toward children.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

Because ExpiryMate's user-created data is stored locally and is not transmitted to developer-operated servers, the application minimizes the amount of data exposed to external systems.

Device-level protection, backup security, and physical device security remain controlled by Android and the user.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ExpiryMate's functionality or data-handling practices change.

Any updated version will include a revised "Last updated" date.
